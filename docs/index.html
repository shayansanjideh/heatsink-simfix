Heat sink simulator-fix eval · round 2 · 60 runs

The best models knew what not to touch

Each model got a heat sink simulator with one missing piece of physics (air leaking around the fins through the gap above them) and 12 bench measurements. Its job: fix the simulator so it predicts 30 held-out designs, including fin counts and fan speeds it never saw. Same 5 setups and 2 attempts for every model. The leak strength was different in each setup, so a textbook default doesn't work.

Prediction error per run

Each dot is one attempt: RMSE in °C over its 30 held-out designs. The vertical tick is the model's mean. For scale: fitting the one leak coefficient and changing nothing else scores 0.92; an unchanged simulator scores 25.

Where the errors come from

Prediction minus reference for every held-out design, across all 10 attempts. Shaded: the fin counts the model saw in its 12 measurements (6 to 26). Errors beyond ±25 °C are pinned to the edge as red triangles. Hover a dot for details. One Terra run and one Luna run crashed on 13 and 12 designs; those are scored as 50 °C misses in the numbers but not plotted.
