    Fan vs. fins

    The fan pushes less air as resistance rises. Airflow settles where the fan curve meets the fins' resistance curve.

    What's computing this

    Textbook correlations, not CFD: Teertstra's channel heat transfer model, Muzychka–Yovanovich duct friction with entrance and exit losses, the fan's pressure–flow curve, fin efficiency, and Song–Lee–Au spreading resistance. Each design takes well under a millisecond, so everything updates as you drag.

    How the colors are drawn

    Fin colors follow the 1D fin equation, T(z) = Tair + (Troot − Tair)·cosh(m(H − z))/cosh(mH). The color scale runs from the 35 °C inlet to the chip temperature. The base plate glow is an approximation of heat spreading out from the chip. Air streaks slow down as the gaps choke.

    What it leaves out

    Fully ducted flow (no air bypassing the fins), uniform air properties, no radiation, and laminar-to-developing flow only. Good for trends and first sizing; a real design still gets a CFD run or a wind-tunnel test.
